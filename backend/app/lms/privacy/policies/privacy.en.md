# Privacy policy

This text is a draft awaiting legal review.

## What we hold
- Your account: email, name, phone if you gave one, language, age band.
- Your courses: enrollments, answers, grades, hand-ins, feedback from teachers.
- Your choices: which policies you accepted and when, and your data requests.

## Why
To run your courses and exams, show your results to you and your teachers, and keep the service safe. We do not sell your data and show no advertising.

## Who sees it
Your teachers and the administrators of your course's organization see your work in that course. A parent you link can see a summary. Our service providers host the data: Vercel (website), Render (server, Frankfurt), Neon (database), Cloudflare R2 (files) and an email provider.

## Your rights
On the Privacy page you can download your data and ask to delete your account.

Deleting removes your name, email, phone, hand-in text and files, and notes about you. Your answers and grades stay without your name, because exam records cannot be changed and course statistics depend on them. Deleted data stays in database backups until they expire.

## Children
Under 14, a parent must consent before the account can be used.
