# Terms of use

This text is a draft awaiting legal review.

## Your account
Use your own account and keep your password safe. Give a true age band. You are responsible for what is done with your account.

## Courses and exams
Teachers and organizations own their courses and decide who joins and how work is graded. Do your own work and do not share exam content.

## What you upload
Upload only what you have the right to share. We may remove content that breaks the law or these terms.

## Availability
We work to keep the service running but cannot promise it is always available. An open timed exam keeps its saved answers if the connection drops.

## Closing an account
You can ask to delete your account on the Privacy page. We may disable an account that breaks these terms.

## Changes
If these terms change in a way that needs your agreement, we will ask you again.
