# Parent consent

This text is a draft awaiting legal review.

I am the parent or legal guardian of this child and I am at least 18 years old.

I agree that the child may use the platform under the privacy policy and the terms of use, and that the platform holds the child's account, course work, answers and grades as they describe.

I understand that:
- I can see a summary of the child's courses and grades. I cannot see their answers or take part in their courses.
- I can withdraw consent or ask to delete the child's account. Without consent, the child's account is restricted.
- Exams already in progress are never interrupted.
