# ORT player and results: UX audit (2026-10-24)

Source: `docs/phases/PHASE_6_screens/audit/student-{14..31,90}-*.jpg` (phone 375 px, desktop 1440 px; en/ky/ru). Standard: `docs/UX_GUIDELINES.md` §2, §3, §5, §6. Code: `frontend/src/components/lms/player/**`, `components/lms/results/**`, `components/lms/NumberedPassage.tsx`, `components/lms/assessments/LobbyView.tsx`.

**What works well (keep):** the exam screen is calm (question, answers, timer, palette, save state, nothing else); the comparison card, KaTeX, section timers, forward-only flow and autosave all work; the save pill already has saving / saved / offline / error states with Retry; dialogs count unanswered and flagged questions and say "cannot return"; the timer is server-corrected and announces the 5-minute and 1-minute marks to screen readers; review cards mark correct/incorrect with color, icon and text; phone layouts have no cut text in any language inside the player itself.

Severity: **blocker** (cannot finish / data risk), **major** (confusing, slow, inaccessible), **minor** (polish).

### Cross-cutting (design system)

- **Colors**: three alert tones inside one dialog (red "no return", amber bullets, blue primary); keep red only for the irreversible sentence. Golden banner for achievements is correct (`OrtResultView.tsx:21-26`).
- **Type**: pick one family (P7). Phone body text is 16 px (ok); the palette legend is 11 px (`Palette.tsx:64`) — raise to 12 px.
- **Spacing/components**: the player and results re-style raw buttons and a local `Modal`; Phase 6 should swap them for `Button`, `Dialog`, `Skeleton`, `Badge`, `Table` without changing the layout.
- **States**: loading (P11) and crash (B1) are the two missing states; empty and error states exist.
- **Dates**: B3 affects every screen.

## Part 2. Ideas that change how the exam works (owner decides)

| # | What would change | Pros | Cons | How it differs from the real ORT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| O1 | A "Раздел 2 начинается. Нажмите Начать" screen between sections; the next section's timer starts on that press, not immediately after closing the previous one (`PlayerShell.tsx:416-418`). | Natural pause, no lost seconds while the student reads the new instructions, less panic. | Students could stretch the exam indefinitely; total time no longer predictable. | In the real ORT sections follow the proctor's command with no student-controlled pause. |
| O2 | Show all five sections in the palette, closed ones greyed (today only the current section is listed in forward-only mode). | Overall progress is visible ("section 2 of 5" becomes tangible). | More to look at; closed sections cannot be opened, which may tempt taps. | The paper booklet contains all sections, but the student may only work in the current one; the header already says "Раздел 1 из 5". |
| O3 | Strike-through (eliminate) answer options with a long press / small "x". | Mirrors crossing out on paper; speeds up guessing strategies. | New interaction to learn; stored per attempt (new data). | Possible on paper, not an on-screen feature of the real exam. |
| O4 | Auto-advance to the next question after choosing an answer (optional setting). | Fewer taps on a phone. | Accidental taps move the student on; changes the rhythm of the test. | Not how the paper test works. |
| O5 | Total exam time remaining in the header next to the section timer. | Helps plan the 215 minutes. | Two clocks compete for attention; real students only see the section clock. | The real ORT has only per-section time. |
| O6 | Scaled score shown for a section with raw 0 is "0" on a 60-245 scale (`student-24-result-ort-*`); either show "—" / "60" or keep the current `zero_raw` rule. | A "0" next to "Шкала 60–245" looks like a bug to students. | Changing the display touches the scoring presentation, which is owner-defined data. | The official certificate never shows a section below the scale minimum. |
| O7 | Vibration (phone) at the 5-minute and 1-minute marks. | Not color-only; works with the phone face down. | Guidelines say no sounds in exams; vibration is borderline and not available on desktop. | No such signal in the paper exam. |
| O8 | Swipe left/right between questions on the phone. | Fast one-handed navigation. | Accidental swipes while scrolling long passages. | Navigation freedom inside a section is the same; only the gesture is new. |
| O9 | Let the student pause an in-progress attempt (stop the clock). | Phone calls, poor network. | Breaks timing integrity; opens cheating paths. | Impossible in the real ORT. |
